\subsection{Visual Hull Initialization}
\label{sec:visual_hull}

Under sparse-view settings, traditional Structure-from-Motion (SfM)~\cite{ref24} often produces sparse and incomplete point clouds because reliable multi-view geometric constraints are limited. Since 3D Gaussian Splatting relies on the initial point distribution to initialize Gaussian primitives, insufficient geometric coverage can destabilize the subsequent optimization. We therefore construct a Visual Hull prior from sparse multi-view images, camera parameters, and foreground masks. The masks are lightly post-processed to suppress boundary noise. Using the known cameras, the foreground silhouettes are back-projected into 3D, and their multi-view silhouette consistency defines a coarse geometric envelope of the target~\cite{ref26,ref27}.

Candidate 3D points are projected into all valid views and retained only when their projections are consistent with the corresponding foreground masks. The retained points form the Visual Hull baseline point cloud, followed by a one-time statistical and radius-based filtering step to remove isolated outliers~\cite{ref29}. This point cloud provides a stable but coarse geometric scaffold for subsequent depth-guided densification and Gaussian initialization. However, silhouette-based reconstruction cannot fully recover concave regions and fine structures. We therefore further introduce \textbf{VGGT / Depth-Guided Densification} (Sec.~\ref{sec:depth_densify}) to supplement the missing geometry before initializing the 3D Gaussian primitives.
