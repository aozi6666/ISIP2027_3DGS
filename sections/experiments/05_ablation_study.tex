\subsection{Ablation Study}
\label{sec:ablation}

\input{tables/ablation}

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} isolates the contributions of the three components under the same training settings. Visual Hull Initialization establishes the geometric initialization baseline, yielding a PSNR of 17.51~dB. Adding Depth Densification increases PSNR to 24.96~dB, representing the largest performance change in the ablation and indicating the importance of depth-guided point supplementation. Adding DVR further improves the complete configuration to 26.18~dB, with corresponding gains in SSIM and LPIPS. Across normalized distance intervals, SynGS also keeps higher average gradient magnitudes than the baseline, most clearly in the $0.8$--$1.0$ range (about $0.2$ vs.\ $1.2$), indicating stronger supervision on distant, weakly visible primitives. The full SynGS configuration achieves the best combined result in the table.
